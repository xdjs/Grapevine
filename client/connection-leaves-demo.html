🍃 Connection Leaves Demo

This demo shows how the connection leaves will look and behave in your network visualization. The leaves are positioned on the connection lines and are clickable with hover effects.

  • Connection Lines: Blue lines connecting the nodes (like in your network)
  • Connection Leaves: Green circles positioned on the connection lines

  • Interactive: Click any leaf to see it in action

🔧 Implementation Details

This demo shows exactly how the connection leaves will appear in your network visualization:

  • Positioning: Leaves are mathematically positioned on the connection lines
  • Spacing: Evenly distributed at 1/3 and 2/3 along each line
    • No Floating: Leaves are anchored to the connection lines
  • Responsive: Leaves move with the connection lines
  • Clickable: Each leaf has its own click event handler
  • Styled: Green color with hover effects matching your design
